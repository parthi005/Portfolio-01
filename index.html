Hardware / Automation

Dual-Axis Solar Tracking System

Designed and implemented a smart, automated dual-axis tracking system using Arduino Nano to align solar panels with the sun's position throughout the day. By utilizing LDR sensors and precision servomotors, this system improves energy generation efficiency by ~35% compared to fixed-angle solar panels. Modeled and validated using MATLAB/Simulink.
